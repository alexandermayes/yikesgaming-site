Big Yikes Bot

A community assistant for Big Yikes, with Forever first and Classic MoP alongside it.

For members

Use Finish setup in get-perms to request Forever, Classic MoP, both, or Friends access. Guild requests need one main class and your main character’s name. An officer still reviews your request.

Use My requests to check your status or cancel a pending request. You may receive a setup reminder or a notice when your guild rank changes. If the bot cannot confirm an action, contact an officer before submitting it again.

For officers

Review requests through the bot’s private Discord workflow. The bot checks the current member, reviewed permissions and approval scope before applying a decision. The management dashboard provides scoped request views; Zev can also save private drafts there. Publishing and approvals stay in Discord.

Help and privacy

Contact Zev on Discord through the Big Yikes server for help, reports of misuse, or requests to access, correct or delete your bot-held information. A moderator can route a request to Zev.
