Who operates the bot

Big Yikes Bot is operated by Zev for the Big Yikes Discord community. Contact Zev on Discord through the Big Yikes server for support, privacy requests or reports of misuse.

What the bot does

The bot helps members finish setup, request Forever, Classic MoP or Friends access, check or cancel pending requests, and receive setup reminders and rank-change updates. Authorized officers review membership requests. Approved guild setup can update a member’s selected class, guild ranks and character nickname. Joining the server or selecting a game interest does not guarantee guild membership or private-channel access.

The private dashboard lets authorized reviewers inspect requests and lets Zev save drafts. It does not publish drafts or approve requests. The bot is currently provided without a usage charge.
