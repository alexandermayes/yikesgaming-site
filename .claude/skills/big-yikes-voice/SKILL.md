# Big Yikes voice

Big Yikes is a casual WoW guild of about 400 people that also runs Yikesheim, a Valheim server. The voice is a
guildmate in Discord voice chat: dry, self-aware about wiping, practical when someone needs to get something working.

## Lines that are already ours (match these)

- "We wipe. We laugh. We pull again."
- "Bing and Beti approve this website." / "Bing and Beti are on it."
- "Existing characters get turned away at the door."
- "Valhalla trips" (deaths), "first to reach the Swamp", "has fallen"
- "Close Valheim first, then pick one."
- "Nobody shows up in endgame gear."

## Rules

1. Say the thing, then stop. One idea per sentence; most sentences under 16 words.
2. Name things the way the game or Discord names them: Join IP, Connect, Start modded, #valheim, Recent tab.
   Never invent a feature name when the game already has one.
3. Instructions are steps with the exact clicks: "Steam › right-click Valheim › Manage › Browse local files".
4. Jokes come from the guild's real life (wipes, loot drama, Bing's face), never from wordplay about the product.
   At most one joke per screen, and never inside an error or an instruction.
5. Errors say what happened and what to do next, in the interface's voice: "Passwords are case-sensitive. Copy it
   straight from the pinned message in #valheim." No apologies, no "Oops".
6. Buttons say what happens: "Join the Discord", "Post the join card", "Show 24 more settings". Never "Submit",
   "Get started", "Learn more".
7. Numbers are real and specific ("about 400 of us", "+100% drops", "40 min → 20 min"), never "tons" or "lots".
8. Sentence case. No all-caps labels, no exclamation marks except inside quoted game text.
9. Big Yikes is a guild. Say "guild" and "guildies", never "clan", in game copy, Discord posts and the site.

## Banned words and moves

unleash, elevate, seamless, journey, epic adventure, level up (as a metaphor), dive in, embark, ultimate, vibrant,
next-level, game-changer, "Welcome to the…", "Whether you're X or Y…", "It's not just X, it's Y", em-dash asides,
"—" chains, rhetorical questions as headings, emoji as icons.

## Before shipping copy

- Read it as a guildie on a phone who just clicked a Discord link: is the next action obvious in one glance?
- Cut every sentence that doesn't help someone join, play or find their people.
- Never put the server password in the public site repo; point to the pinned post in #valheim.
